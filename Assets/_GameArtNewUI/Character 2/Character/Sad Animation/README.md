# Sad / Disappointed Animation

A soft, child-friendly 16-frame loop: her smile fades, she lowers her head and clasps her hands, takes a small sigh, then looks up with a gentle hopeful expression. No tears or frightening expressions.

The final five frames reuse the last action pose, adding subtle breathing and one blink. Each cell remains 384 × 512 pixels; the unused final three cells are transparent. The preview holds these idle frames longer.

- `1_Sad_Disappointed_Animation.png`: 21-frame sprite sheet, 4 columns × 6 rows, 1536 × 3072 PNG with transparent background; each frame cell is 384 × 512 pixels.
- `1_Sad_Disappointed_Animation_Preview.gif`: looping preview of the frames in sequence.

Add future sad/disappointed variants with the next number (for example, `2_Sad_Disappointed_Animation.png`).
