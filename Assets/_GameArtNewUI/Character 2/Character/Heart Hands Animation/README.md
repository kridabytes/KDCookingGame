# Heart Hands Animation

An 8-frame loop where the chef forms a heart with her hands, tilts her head, and finishes with a tiny floating pink heart.

The final five frames reuse the last action pose, adding subtle breathing and one blink. Each cell remains 384 × 512 pixels; the unused final three cells are transparent. The preview holds these idle frames longer.

- `1_Heart_Hands.png`: 13-frame sprite sheet, 4 columns × 4 rows, 1536 × 2048 PNG with transparent background.
- `1_Heart_Hands_Preview.gif`: looping preview of the frames in sequence.
