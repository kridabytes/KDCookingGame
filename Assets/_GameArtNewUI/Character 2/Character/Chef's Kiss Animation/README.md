# Chef's Kiss Animation

The chef brings her fingertips to her lips, kisses them, and flicks her hand outward into a delighted open palm. Tiny pink sparkles appear near her hand in the final two frames.

The final five frames reuse the last action pose, adding subtle breathing and one blink. Each cell remains 384 × 512 pixels; the unused final three cells are transparent. The preview holds these idle frames longer.

- `1_Chefs_Kiss.png`: 21-frame sprite sheet, 4 columns × 6 rows, 1536 × 3072 PNG with transparent background; each frame cell is 384 × 512 pixels.
- `1_Chefs_Kiss_Preview.gif`: looping preview of the frames in sequence.
