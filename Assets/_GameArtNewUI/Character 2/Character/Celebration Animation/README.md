# Celebration Animation

The celebration sequence has 19 frames. The hands-down sprite from row 4, column 2 was removed. The remaining action frames end on the raised-open-hands pose; the final five frames continue that pose with subtle breathing and one natural blink.

- `1_Celebration_Animation.png`: 19-frame sprite sheet in a 4-column × 5-row grid, 1536 × 2560 PNG with transparent background. The final cell is empty; every cell is 384 × 512 pixels.
- `1_Celebration_Animation_Preview.gif`: looping preview of all 19 frames. The five idle frames are held longer.
