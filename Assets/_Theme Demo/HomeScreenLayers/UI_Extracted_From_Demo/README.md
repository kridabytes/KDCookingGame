# UI sprites extracted from the home screen demo

These are crops made from the approved `Demos/Theme Upgrade Demo/home-screen-theme-demo.png`; the art was not redrawn or restyled. Transparent pixels around the controls were isolated from the screenshot.

- `01_Settings_Button_From_Demo.png`: settings tile with gear icon
- `02_No_Ads_Button_From_Demo.png`: full No Ads badge with chef hat and crossed-ad icon
- `03_Play_Button_From_Demo.png`: full green Play button with label and play triangle
- `04_Top_Panel_From_Demo.png`: complete top HUD panel as seen in the demo (includes the baked-in 10, 150, and 0 counters and plus buttons)
- `05_Energy_Capsule_From_Demo.png`: energy capsule as shown (includes bolt, 10, and plus button)
- `06_Energy_Icon_From_Demo.png`: bolt icon crop

All PNGs have Unity Sprite (2D and UI) metadata with mipmaps disabled. Since these are extracted from a flattened demo image, the panel text and counters are baked into those two top HUD crops.
